# Getting the ledger on your phone and laptop

No downloads from an app store, no accounts, no passwords. You open a link once and
add it to your device. After that it opens like any other app and works with no signal.

Whoever set this up gave you a web address. Keep it handy — you need it once per device.

---

## iPhone

**Use Safari for this.** Other browsers work for reading the page but the install step is
more reliable in Safari.

1. Open the link in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button — the square with an arrow pointing up out of it, at the bottom of
   the screen.
3. Scroll down the list and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add** in the top right.

You now have a ledger icon on your home screen.

**From now on, open it from that icon, not from Safari.** This isn't just for convenience.
iPhones erase saved data for websites you haven't visited in a week. Apps added to the home
screen are exempt from that rule, so opening it from the icon is what keeps your claims from
disappearing.

---

## Mac

**If you use Safari:**

1. Open the link in Safari.
2. In the menu bar at the top of the screen, click **File**, then **Add to Dock**.
3. Click **Add**.

**If you use Chrome or Edge:**

1. Open the link.
2. Look at the right-hand end of the address bar for a small icon of a screen with a downward
   arrow. Click it, then click **Install**.
3. If you don't see it, open the three-dot menu and look for **Install** or **Cast, save and
   share → Install page as app**.

**One thing to do in the right order:** on a Mac, the installed app keeps its information
completely separate from your browser. So install it *first*, then enter or import your
expenses inside the installed app. If you do it the other way round, your data stays behind in
the browser tab and the app opens empty.

---

## Windows

1. Open the link in Chrome or Edge.
2. Click the install icon at the right of the address bar — a screen with a downward arrow —
   then click **Install**.
3. It'll appear in your Start menu like any other program.

---

## Android

1. Open the link in Chrome.
2. Tap the three-dot menu, then **Add to Home screen**, then **Install**.

---

## Things worth knowing

**Each device is its own copy.** Your phone and your laptop don't talk to each other. If you
add a claim on your phone it won't show up on the laptop. Pick one device as the real one, or
move data across with the backup file described below.

**Back it up now and then.** Open **Setup** and tap **Download a backup**. That saves a file
you can keep in your cloud drive or email to yourself. If you ever get a new phone, or clear
your browsing data by accident, that file is what saves you. The backup covers the ledger
itself — any EOB documents you attached stay on the device, so keep the original PDFs
somewhere too.

**Don't use private or incognito browsing.** Nothing you enter will be saved.

**Reading EOB documents needs a key.** Importing your spreadsheet and all the calculations work
straight away. The feature that reads a PDF or a photo of a statement needs an Anthropic API
key pasted into Setup — whoever set this up can tell you whether you have one and what it
means for your privacy.

**It's not tax advice.** Check anything you submit against your actual plan documents.
